# WhiteLyin

Software, websites and business systems built around real work.

WhiteLyin (Pty) Ltd is a South African technology company developing
software applications, websites and connected business systems.

Our work brings together development, integration and day-to-day
operations to make digital systems useful and maintainable.

## Areas of work

- **Software development:** business applications, portals and workflow automation.
- **Web development:** websites, web applications and digital services.
- **Integration and operations:** connected systems, automation and operational tooling.
